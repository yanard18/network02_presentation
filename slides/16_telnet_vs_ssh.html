Secure Alternative: Telnet vs. SSH

Hardening steps to eliminate cleartext remote access.

  • Decommission Daemons: Stop and purge telnetd across servers, switches, & IoT nodes.
  • Firewall Filtering: Block inbound traffic on TCP 23 & TCP 2323.
  • Deploy SSH Keys: Mandate SSH key pairs (ed25519) and disable password auth.
  • VLAN Isolation: Isolate legacy management interfaces on dedicated, restricted VLANs.
