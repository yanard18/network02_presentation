SNMP Protocol Fundamentals

Remote telemetry, device health monitoring, and administrative configuration.

ARCHITECTURE Core Concepts
  • Purpose: Monitors hardware health & configures network nodes.
  • Architecture: SNMP Manager (NMS) polls SNMP Agents.
  • UDP 161: Active queries (GET / SET) & responses.
  • UDP 162: Unprompted event alerts (SNMP Traps).
  • Ecosystem: Routers, switches, firewalls, servers, & modems.
